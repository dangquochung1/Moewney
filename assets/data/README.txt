Content JSON lives here: enemies, weapons, relics, items, loot_tables,
floors, icons, the village shop (upgrades.json), the village economy
(village.json), the island's festival and rain (events.json), the chess
boards round the island (chess.json), the village's cats (cats.json), the animals
that fight beside the ninja (pets.json) and the dictionary (codex.json). Written by hand, read by
data/ContentLoader, ShopCatalog, VillageCatalog and CodexCatalog, checked by
ContentValidator at startup.
